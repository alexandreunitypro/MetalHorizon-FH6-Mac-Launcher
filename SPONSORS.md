# Sponsoring

MetalHorizon is free and open source, and it stays that way. Sponsoring pays for the time and the hardware that
getting the Xbox edition of Forza Horizon 6 right on a Mac takes.

## The rules

- Every build is free and public, the same day, for everyone. No early access, no paid builds.
- No feature is ever locked behind a sponsorship.
- No game content behind a paywall: no sponsor-only videos or game files.
- Sponsoring never buys help with piracy, cheating or getting around the game's protection.

## Goal: $100/month

What the money goes to, in this order:

1. **Apple Developer Program ($99/year).** A signed and notarized launcher, so macOS opens it without a
   Gatekeeper warning.
2. **A used 8–16 GB Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or M2).** "Will it run on my Mac?" is the most asked question. Today
   the only test machine is an M5 Pro with 48 GB.
3. **Time**: frame pacing, the black foliage, crashes, then a launcher that works on any Mac.

A short public note each month says what came in and where it went.
