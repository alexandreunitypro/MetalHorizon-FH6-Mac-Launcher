**Early alpha.** One step less on Steam, a sign-in that no longer hangs, a new toolbar and an industrial graphite theme. MetalHorizon 0.4 and 0.5 update themselves to this version when they open.

### Steam: the game's install window opens by itself
- Once you have signed in in Steam's window, MetalHorizon opens Steam's own install window for Forza Horizon 6 (`steam://install/2483190`). No need to look for the game in the Library: click **Install** there. Steam checks your licence and downloads the game, as it always does.
- If you are already signed in and the game is not installed, the big button says **Install the Game** and opens that same window.
- Only `steam://` links are handed to Steam.

### Xbox
- **Sign-in no longer hangs on "Signing In…"**: the sign-in ends as soon as Microsoft's page reports it is done, even when its window stays open in the background.
- **"Invalid profile" (E:43-1b-4)** is now recognized and explained: it comes from the Forza profile on Microsoft's side, not from the Mac.

### Industrial look
- Graphite and black, no colour: the Play button, the road to Play, the haze behind the glass and the links.

### The toolbar
- The edition switch is two short, equal segments (Xbox, Steam), centred; the Activity button sits at the right edge; the repeated window title is gone.

### Xbox (from 0.5.0)
- Right after you sign in with Microsoft, the game's download starts by itself when the disk has room for it (about 170 GB).

### Still yours
Signing in to Microsoft or Steam (MetalHorizon never sees your password), accepting Apple's licence for D3DMetal, and clicking Install in Steam's window: those are your accounts and your licences.
