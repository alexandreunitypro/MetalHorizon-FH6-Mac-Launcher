**The Xbox sign-in is fixed at its root** (issues #1, #9 and #13). MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens.

### "Initialization Failed", E:0-c2-4, E:0-0-L-8, "Welcome back, Player" with no save
- **The cause**: Microsoft now hands out the Xbox delegation ticket as a compact JWE, which Xbox Live wants sent as `t=`. The download program already did that; the Xbox service that runs beside the game still sent `d=`, so every request for the account's Xbox token got HTTP 400 (`Failed to authenticate Xbox user`, dozens of times in `xodus-service.log`). The game then had no Xbox identity. Signing in again could not fix it.
- **The fix**: the Xbox service in this version sends the ticket in the right form (the same patch as the download program, with its tests). Thank you to KFZ1804 and sshubhayu, who found and confirmed it.
- **Nothing to do**: no new sign-in. At the next Play, MetalHorizon replaces a service left running by an older version, or one Xbox had already refused, with a fresh one.
- **One thing macOS may ask, once**: the Xbox service is a new program for the Keychain, so macOS can show "xodus-service wants to use your confidential information stored in Xodus Service". Choose **Always Allow** (for each window). Play now says so under its button and waits for your answer; before, it gave up after a minute and started a game with no Xbox account.
- **Play no longer starts the game without the Xbox service**: if the service cannot start, Play stops and says what to do, instead of leading to Initialization Failed.
- The diagnosis names these cases if they ever show again.

### The account is checked before the game starts
- **A real test, without the game**: after every sign-in and before every Play, MetalHorizon asks its Xbox service to sign in to Xbox Live for real, the way the game's requests go. Accepted: Play goes on. Refused: Play stops there, says what Xbox answered, and the button becomes **Sign In Again**, instead of a game that starts and says Initialization Failed.
- **The Xbox service no longer falls over on a refusal**: it tries three times, a few seconds apart, logs what Xbox or Microsoft actually answered (the reports only ever showed "Status(400, None)"), and keeps serving the game's other requests.
- Checked here against Xbox Live with a real account: the service in this version is accepted on the path that was refused before.
