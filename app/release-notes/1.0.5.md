**Steam edition: "Initialization Failed" (E:0-0-L-8) after Steam starts the game.** MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens.

### The cause
- Since 1.0.4 Steam starts the game itself, as it must, yet the game still stopped at Initialization Failed (E:0-0-L-8): the report of 20 November (M1 Pro, macOS 27.0.1) shows Steam starting the game, the game running five minutes, and no Xbox sign-in.
- The Steam edition of Forza Horizon 6 signs in to Xbox **by itself**, with its own sign-in library, as on a Windows PC without the Xbox app: the first time it shows a code to enter at **microsoft.com/link**, then keeps the sign-in in Windows' credential store.
- But Blackwell Engine carries an Xbox runtime (xgameruntime) made for the **Xbox edition**, whose sign-in goes through MetalHorizon's xodus-service. The Steam game found it, let it take over the sign-in, and that runtime, with no service behind it on Steam, said someone was signed in and then had no Xbox token to give: E:0-0-L-8. Highball's engine, where the Steam game signs in, has no such runtime at all.

### The fix
- The Xbox runtime is now off for everything in the Steam prefix (`xgameruntime=d`). Checked on Blackwell Engine steam-1.2: a program in a Steam prefix loaded it before, and finds no such library now (error 126), as in Highball's engine.
- **The first time**, the game shows a sign-in code: go to **microsoft.com/link** on any device, sign in with your Microsoft account and enter the code. The game keeps the sign-in from then on.
