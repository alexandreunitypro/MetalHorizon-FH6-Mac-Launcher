**Steam sign-in fixed (E:0-0-L-8), and a beta engine against the freezes every ten seconds.** MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens.

### Steam edition: "Initialization Failed" (E:0-0-L-8) after Steam starts the game
- The Steam edition of Forza Horizon 6 signs in to Xbox **by itself**: the first time it shows a code to enter at **microsoft.com/link**, then keeps the sign-in.
- Blackwell Engine carried an Xbox runtime made for the Xbox edition (its sign-in is MetalHorizon's xodus-service). The Steam game found it, let it take the sign-in over, and with no service behind it on Steam it had no Xbox token to give: E:0-0-L-8 (issues #9, #13, #16, #17, and the report of 20 November). It is now off in the Steam prefix, as in Highball's engine, where the Steam game signs in. Checked on Blackwell Engine steam-1.2: a program in the Steam prefix loaded it before, and finds no such library now.

### Beta engine (alpha): Settings › Advanced › Beta engine
Off by default. Turned on, MetalHorizon installs **Blackwell Engine Beta** beside the public engine (about 100 MB) and plays it on D3DMetal; turned off, it removes it and the public engine plays again, as before.

- **The Blackwell Pacer.** Forza Horizon 6's copy protection rewrites its own code all the time; under Rosetta the translator's store fills, and Rosetta empties it in one go while every thread that needs it waits: the freeze of a tenth of a second or more every ten seconds or so. Mathias Kowoll (MacGameFix) found it and its countermeasure, a thread that runs large functions never seen before. The pacer is Blackwell Engine's own take on it: it lives in Wine's ntdll, runs only in the game and only under Rosetta, and **tunes itself to your Mac** instead of a number to pick.
- Measured on an M5 Pro, without the game, on a load that does what the protection does: inside Wine, 3 freezes of 197-306 ms a minute without the pacer, **none** with it; on the macOS bench, none for 10-14 % of the load's throughput, where MacGameFix's fixed 40 % costs 26-30 %. These are bench numbers, **not measured in the game**: please report what you see (Activity › Export Diagnostics; the game's log carries a `bw-pacer:` line every ten seconds).
- **Five of Highball's engine fixes** not in the public engine: msync's waits (0005, 0014), audio pulls under half a period (0015), thread-pool I/O freed once (0025), a fault on a foreign thread ends the program instead of hanging it (0028).
- The Xbox edition and the Steam edition each get their own beta engine. The native engine (FEX) is not changed: the pacer is for Rosetta.
