**MetalHorizon X 2.0: Forza Horizon 6 in 34 languages, a real download bar, and a black-glass window made for the Mac.** MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens.

### Speaks your language
MetalHorizon follows the language of your Mac, with nothing to set: 33 languages besides English, from Arabic to Vietnamese, the window, Settings, the menus, the activity log and the first-launch guide. A language without a translation shows in English.

### See the download, in numbers
The Xbox download shows a bar that fills as the game arrives: the percentage, the amount downloaded out of the total (for example "12.3 GB of 158 GB"), the speed and the time left. A resumed or updated download counts only what it fetches. An older download tool, or the first seconds before the numbers come, keep the bar moving without numbers.

### A window in black glass
The whole window is deep black glass with a studio light above the title and a faint reflection under it. The Xbox / Steam switch lights the copy you chose; the activity log is a narrow panel that slides in on the right, with a search, an errors-only filter and the Game Output tab. The top bar is gone: the window starts at its top edge.

### Also in this version
- The Beta engine (alpha) and the Steam fixes from 1.1.0 are unchanged.
- The app is called MetalHorizon X. Its data and your settings stay where they were.

### Known limits
- The detailed messages (diagnostics, repairs, the Doctor's checks) are in English for now.
- The layout of the Arabic and Hebrew screens is being checked: some titles still align to the left.
- The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized yet: macOS asks you to confirm the first launch.
