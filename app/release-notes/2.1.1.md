**MetalHorizon X 2.1.1: frame generation fixed, your profile picture, English on an English Mac, and the GameInput dispatcher for controllers.** MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens, and installs Blackwell Engine 1.3.1 by itself.

Thank you for the reports on 2.1.0. One of them confirmed in the game that the **VIP membership is recognised and the Car Pass cars work** (issue #18).

### Frame generation (MetalDuck), fixed
- On some Macs the overlay drew the game in a corner of the screen and stayed on "MetalDuck: loading". There were two causes.
  - **Window sizes.** Apple's model takes only some sizes, with no rule it states. On an M5 Pro it takes 1728×1116 but never 1790×1158, the shape of a 16-inch MacBook Pro's screen. MetalHorizon now tries sizes on the model before anything is shown, uses the first one it takes, and remembers it for the next game.
  - **Layout.** The overlay's picture now follows the game's window size.
- If the model takes no size, nothing is shown over the game, and it plays as it would without frame generation.
- What it tried and used is in Activity › Export Diagnostics (`frame-generation.log`).

### Your profile picture
- The picture's address from your Xbox profile already carries its own format. MetalHorizon added a second one, and the image service then answered something else, so the grey square stayed.
- The address is now completed properly and asked over https. An answer that is not a PNG (a JPEG, for example) is converted to one by macOS itself.

### Language
- MetalHorizon X had no English among its languages, so a Mac set to English could show it in another language, German for one player. English is there now.
- **Settings › General › Language** lets you choose any language, whatever your Mac's.

### Controllers (Blackwell Engine 1.3.1)
- GameInput's dispatcher was missing ("not implemented"). A game that makes a dispatcher, to run GameInput's callbacks itself, could see no pad at all, wired or not. It is there now, as on Windows. Checked without the game, on Blackwell Engine 1.3.1 and Native 1.3.1 (`engine/tests/gameinput-pads`, 20 of 20 checks).
- If your pad still does nothing in the game, please try **Settings › Controllers › Off** once and tell us in issue #15 whether it works then. That tells us which layer is at fault.

### Beta engine 1.4a2
Blackwell Engine 1.3.1 with the beta's work against the Rosetta hitches (Blackwell Pacer, Trap Boost, Highball's five Rosetta-line patches), as in 1.4a1.

### About the stutter on Rosetta
- `MGF_FH6_ROSETTA_PACER` is MacGameFix's setting: MetalHorizon's engines do not read it, so it changes nothing here. MetalHorizon's own pacer is in the beta engine (Settings › Advanced › Beta engine): it tunes itself, with nothing to set.
- The pacer shortens the long freezes (every ten seconds or so). The hitch about once a second comes from the game's copy protection under Rosetta. The beta's Trap Boost shortens it, and the Xbox edition's native engine has none.
- The Steam edition runs on Rosetta only for now.

### A sturdier download and Xbox sign-in (Xbox edition)
- The download no longer gives up on the first network hiccup. It tries again with growing pauses and moves to another of the game's servers. An interrupted download picks up where it stopped instead of starting from zero. Stop now pauses it.
- A full disk, a sign-in Xbox no longer accepts, a refused Keychain or a wrong clock each get a clear message with what to do, instead of a crash or "Download finished." with no game.
- The Mac stays awake while the game downloads. Without the network, MetalHorizon waits for it to come back, and resumes by itself.
- The full list of 50 changes is in `docs/XODUS-ROBUSTNESS.md`. They were tested without the game: no real download or sign-in has been made with them yet.
