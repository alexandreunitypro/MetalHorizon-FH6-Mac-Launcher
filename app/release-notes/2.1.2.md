**MetalHorizon X 2.1.2: Blackwell Engine 1.4, the beta's work against the Rosetta stutter for everyone.** MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens, and installs Blackwell Engine 1.4 by itself.

### Blackwell Engine 1.4 (Rosetta)
Beta 1.4a2 becomes the public engine. It has the same files, now for every player on Rosetta: the Steam edition, and the Xbox edition when it plays on D3DMetal.
- **The Blackwell Pacer** keeps Rosetta's translator from stopping the game for long (the freezes every ten seconds or so). It tunes itself to the Mac: there is nothing to set.
- **The Trap Boost** shortens the hitch about once a second. That hitch comes from the game's copy protection under Rosetta: the boost raises Rosetta's exception server while the game's traps are handled.
- **Five of Highball's Rosetta-line fixes**, among them one that keeps closing asynchronous I/O from crashing.
- On a Mac with more than 8 processors, the game sees 8: fewer of its threads compete for the translator, and the Mac runs cooler.
- All of it runs only in Forza Horizon 6 and only under Rosetta. The native engine of the Xbox edition, which has no such hitch, is unchanged and still plays on Automatic where it works.
- `MGF_FH6_ROSETTA_PACER` and MacGameFix's other settings are not read: MetalHorizon's engine sets itself.

It was measured without the game (a trap takes 108 µs without the boost, 60 µs with it, M5 Pro) and has not yet been measured in Forza Horizon 6. If the game runs worse than with 2.1.1, please say so in an issue with Activity › Export Diagnostics. The record of the engine that played (`rosetta_line`) is in the zip.
