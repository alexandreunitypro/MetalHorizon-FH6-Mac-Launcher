**MetalHorizon X 2.2.0: the Steam edition signs in to Xbox again, and a window to tell the developer how the game ran, only if you want to.** MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens.

### Steam edition: Xbox sign-in fixed ("Sign In Required", issue #21)
- Since October 2025, the Steam version of Forza Horizon 6 carries its own Xbox sign-in, `xgameruntime.dll` in the game's folder. MetalHorizon 1.1.0 to 2.1.2 kept that file from loading, so the game had no Xbox sign-in at all and showed "Sign In Required". It loads now.
- The first time, the game shows a code. Enter it at microsoft.com/link with your Microsoft account; the game keeps the sign-in after that.
- A Steam client left running by an older MetalHorizon is restarted before the game starts, so that the game gets the new settings.
- Checked with test programs and a stand-in game on Blackwell Engine steam-1.3 and steam-1.4; not yet with the real Steam copy of the game.

### After a game
When Forza Horizon 6 closes, MetalHorizon asks how it ran:
- 5 stars;
- how far the game got (driving in the open world, the main menu, a loading screen, a black screen, or not at all);
- what worked: smoothness, picture, sound, controller, online play, add-ons and VIP, profile picture, frame generation;
- a comment, if you have something to say.

Everything is optional: the stars alone, or Send with nothing, are a report. **Tools › Report a Problem…** and **Rate the Last Game…** do the same at any time, with the logs attached (passwords and identifiers removed) if you keep the box ticked.

### Your choice, kept
**Settings › Feedback** sets what is sent:
- **Ask Me** (the default): nothing leaves your Mac until you click Send.
- **Send Automatically**: for when reports go straight to the developer (see below).
- **Never**: nothing is sent, and MetalHorizon does not ask.

"Don't ask after games" keeps the window from coming back.

### Private by design
- A report never holds your name, your Microsoft or Steam account, your gamertag, your Xbox or Steam ID, or your files.
- The logs also lose your Mac's user name from their paths now (`/Users/<user>`), in Activity › Export Diagnostics too.
- Your Mac is known only by an anonymous ID, which Settings › Feedback renews.
- **In this version, Send opens a pre-filled GitHub issue** for a problem or a comment, with the logs zip shown in Finder to attach. A rating alone stays on your Mac. Sending straight to the developer, without GitHub, comes in an update. Your Mac will then keep each report until the developer's Mac takes it, never while the game runs or downloads, 30 days at most.
