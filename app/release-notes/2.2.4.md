**MetalHorizon X 2.2.4: the Steam edition relaunches the game after D3DMetal's Neural Engine failure again (issue #27).** MetalHorizon updates itself to this version when it opens.

### Steam edition
- D3DMetal can stop in the game's first seconds while it prepares its Neural Engine model ("com.apple.appleneuralengine Code=26 ... Bad argument error"). It depends on timing, so MetalHorizon relaunches the game by itself (twice at most, Options › Relaunch After D3DMetal Start-up Failure).
- On the Steam edition that relaunch could be missed: MetalHorizon took the game for closed the instant no game process was left, before the error reached the log through Steam's output or a second game process took over. It counted its 30 seconds from Steam's start instead of the game's, and Steam's restart for its pre-launch notices used one of the two relaunches.
- Now it waits up to 10 seconds for a game process to come back, times the 30 seconds from the game, and keeps both relaunches for this error. Checked with a simulated game whose process hands over and whose error line arrives late; not yet with the real Steam copy.

Everything else is as in 2.2.3.
